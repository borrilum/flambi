# Starter Presets

1. **Mist Floor** - Soft low drone bed with gentle space.
2. **Rusted Air** - Noisier, gritty air layer, band-pass mood.
3. **Deep Tunnel** - Darker tunnel-like evolving drone.
4. **Hollow Pad** - Mid-focused ghost pad tone.
5. **Fog Chord** - Wider wash for playable chord ambience.
6. **Broken Cloud** - More unstable and textured movement.
7. **Soft Dub Bloom** - Warm dub bloom with deeper space.
8. **Night Rail** - Rolling nocturnal texture with motion.
